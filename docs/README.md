# OctoAcme Project Management Docs

## Overview

OctoAcme follows an iterative, customer-first project management approach designed to deliver value incrementally while maintaining clear ownership, data-informed decisions, and psychological safety across teams. The organization emphasizes structured lifecycle phases, defined roles with clear accountability, and intentional communication rhythms that keep stakeholders aligned and risks visible.

## Core Principles

- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability in every decision
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments to gather feedback
- **Clear ownership**: Each project has a named Project Manager (PM) and Product Lead
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and iterate based on evidence
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage feedback, learning, and continuous improvement

## OctoAcme Project Management Approach

OctoAcme's project management model integrates structured lifecycle phases with cross-functional collaboration and risk management. Projects move through five key phases: **Initiation** (where a one-pager clarifies problem, goals, stakeholders, and initial risks with a decision gate for approval), **Planning** (creating prioritized backlogs, defining acceptance criteria, estimating work, and mapping dependencies), **Execution** (building and testing on regular cadence with quality gates at each step), **Release** (deploying to production with pre-release checklists and post-deploy verification), and **Retrospective** (capturing learnings and converting them into actionable improvements).

The organization defines core roles—Project Managers coordinate delivery and communication, Product Managers own business value and outcome measurement, Developers build and test features, and QA teams validate quality—each with clear responsibilities and success criteria. Communication is treated as an intentional rhythm: weekly PM/Product alignment, regular team standups, milestone-based stakeholder updates, and structured escalation paths ensure transparency and help teams surface issues early. Quality assurance is built into the workflow through small PRs, CI checks, test coverage, security scans, code reviews, and smoke tests before release. Together, these elements create a disciplined approach that balances ownership, communication, risk management, and quality to improve delivery outcomes over time.

## Project Lifecycle

OctoAcme projects follow a structured five-phase lifecycle:

1. **Initiation**: Define the problem, align stakeholders, and make a go/no-go decision
2. **Planning**: Break work into shippable increments and create an actionable backlog
3. **Execution**: Build, test, review, and iterate on a regular cadence
4. **Release**: Deploy to production with confidence and minimal risk
5. **Close & Retrospective**: Capture learnings and drive continuous improvement
